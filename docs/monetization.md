# Monetization Plan

## Launch: Free Policy Gate

Launch `evaluate_action_policy` as a free A2MCP service.

Why:

- Removes payment friction during marketplace review.
- Drives real calls, ratings, and integrations.
- Establishes trust before charging for security decisions.
- Avoids claiming x402 settlement before verified middleware exists.

## Paid Deep Scan

After payment and data-provider integrations:

- Explorer verification.
- Transaction simulation.
- Liquidity depth and pool age.
- Holder concentration.
- Wallet approval/history indexing.
- Threat-intelligence matches.
- Downloadable evidence report.

Indicative pricing:

- Policy gate: `$0.05` per verified call.
- Deep scan: `$0.10-$0.25` per verified call.
- Incident report: `$25-$100`.

## A2A Audit Desk

Custom token, wallet, incident, and agent-policy reports can be offered as a negotiated A2A service after the free utility builds demand.

Indicative pricing:

- Token or wallet research: `$499-$2,500`.
- Integration and policy design: custom.

## Revenue Accounting

- `paymentProofCalls`: requests that presented a payment header; not settlement proof.
- `verifiedPaymentCalls`: requests trusted middleware marked as settled.
- `qualifiedRevenueUsd`: price of verified settled calls only.
- `projectedRevenueUsd`: price projection for metered premium calls; not revenue.
- `blockedActionValueUsd`: caller-declared value on blocked actions; not custody or transaction volume.

Revenue Rocket is not the deadline-day positioning unless real verified orders and reviews already exist.
